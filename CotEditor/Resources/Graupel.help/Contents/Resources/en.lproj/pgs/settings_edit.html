Edit settings

Choose Graupel > Settings, then click Edit.

SettingWhat it does
Prefer using

Whether the Tab key and indentation put in Spaces or Tabs.
Indent width

How many spaces one level of indentation is, and how wide a tab looks.
Detect indent style on document opening

Looks at an opened document’s indented lines and uses spaces or tabs to match, and for spaces, the same width, such as 2. This wins over the settings here and in Mode settings. If too few lines are indented to tell, Graupel uses the settings.
Automatically indent while typing

Starts each new line at the same indentation as the line above.
Indent selection with Tab key

With text selected, Tab indents the selected lines and Shift-Tab outdents them, instead of replacing the selection.
Automatically trim trailing whitespace

Removes spaces and tabs at the ends of lines 3 seconds after you stop typing, except on the lines where you’re typing, and from every line when you save. Including whitespace-only lines also empties lines that hold only spaces or tabs.
Insert comment delimiters after indent

When you comment out lines, puts the comment characters after the indentation instead of at the start of the line.
Add a space to comment delimiters

Puts a space between the comment characters and the code, as in // code.
Link URLs in document

Makes web addresses in the text clickable.
Highlight matching braces

Briefly highlights the matching bracket when the insertion point moves past (, [, {, or their closing partners.
Highlight instances of selected text

When you select a whole word, highlights everywhere else it appears, after the Delay you set in seconds.

To use other indentation settings for code, or for one syntax such as Python, change them in Mode settings.
