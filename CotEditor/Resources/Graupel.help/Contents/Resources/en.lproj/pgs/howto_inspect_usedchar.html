Find characters the encoding can’t save

If a document has characters its encoding can’t store, those characters are replaced or removed when you save. This happens most often with older encodings, such as Western (Mac OS Roman) or Japanese (Shift JIS), which hold far fewer characters than UTF-8.

To see them, choose View > Inspector > Warnings. The Warnings inspector lists each character the encoding can’t store. Click one to go to it in the text.

To close the inspector, choose View > Inspector > Hide Inspector.

To keep every character, save the document in UTF-8 instead. See Save a document in a different encoding.
