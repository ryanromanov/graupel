Split the editor

Split the editor to see two parts of a document at once. Both halves show the same document, so a change in one appears in the other.

  • To split, choose View > Split Editor, or click Split Editor at the right end of the bar above the text.

  • To put the halves side by side or one above the other, choose View > Stack Editors Vertically or View > Stack Editors Horizontally.

  • To close the half you’re in, choose View > Close Split Editor.
