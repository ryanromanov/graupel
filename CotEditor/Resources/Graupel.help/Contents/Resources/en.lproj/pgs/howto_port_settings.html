Move your settings to another Mac

You can save your Graupel settings to a file and import it on another Mac, or keep a copy in iCloud that your other Macs can import. Either way, you choose what to include:

  • Settings: everything in the Settings window, including modes, snippets, and shortcuts.

  • Themes and syntaxes you made or changed.

  • Multiple Replace definitions.

  • File scopes saved for Folder Find.

Scripts aren’t included. To move them, copy the files in the Scripts folder; see Automate tasks with scripting.

Export and import a settings file

  1. Choose Graupel > Settings, then click General.

  2. Next to Settings import & export, click Export…, select what to include, click Export, then choose where to save the file. It’s a Graupel Settings Archive (.cotsettings).

  3. On the other Mac, click Import… in the same place, choose the file, select what to import, then click Import.

Imported items replace any with the same name.

To share one theme, syntax, or replace definition instead, export it on its own. See Change the colors, Make or change a syntax, and Make several replacements at once.

Keep a copy in iCloud

Each Mac must be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on.

  1. On the Mac whose settings you want to keep, choose Graupel > Settings, click General, then select Keep a copy in iCloud. Graupel updates the copy whenever you change one of these settings. Each Mac and each user account on it keeps its own copy.

  2. On the other Mac, click the arrow on the Import… button in the same place, choose the copy, select what to import, then click Import. This works even if Keep a copy in iCloud is off on that Mac.

To stop keeping a copy, deselect Keep a copy in iCloud on the Mac it comes from. The copy stays in iCloud until you delete it: click the arrow on Import…, choose Manage Copies on iCloud…, select the copy, then click Delete Copy. Deleting a copy doesn’t change the settings on any Mac.
