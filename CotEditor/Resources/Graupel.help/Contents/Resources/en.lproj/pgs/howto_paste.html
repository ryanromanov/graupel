Paste text

Choose Edit > Paste or press Command-V. Graupel changes the line endings in the pasted text to match the document’s, so a document doesn’t end up with a mix of line endings.

Paste without changing line endings

Press Command-Option-V, or hold down the Option key and choose Edit > Paste Exactly. The text goes in as it is on the clipboard.

This can leave the document with more than one kind of line ending. To find them, see Find inconsistent line endings.
