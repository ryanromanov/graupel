Regular expressions

A regular expression is a pattern that describes text. For example, dog|cow matches “dog” or “cow”, and \d+ matches any run of digits.

You can use regular expressions in:

  • Find and replace, in the Find & Replace window and in Multiple Replace

  • Folder Find

  • Sort by Pattern

  • Syntax definitions, for highlighting and the outline

Regular expressions differ a little from one program to another. Graupel uses the ICU syntax, which is close to the one in Perl and Python. For the full syntax, see the ICU regular expressions guide. For a short reference while you search, click the question mark next to Regular Expression in the Find & Replace window.

In a replacement, $0 is the whole match and $1, $2, and so on are the captured groups.
