Shift JIS variants

Japanese text is often stored in Shift JIS, which comes in several versions. Graupel has four. Picking the right one avoids characters that change or disappear when you save.

Name in GraupelCharset nameWhat it is
Japanese (Shift JIS)shift_jis

Basic Shift JIS, with JIS level 1 and 2 kanji.

Japanese (Shift JIS X0213)Shift_JIS

Adds the JIS X 0213 characters, including level 3 and 4 kanji.

Japanese (Mac OS)x-mac-japanese

Shift JIS with Mac characters, used by Mac OS 9 and earlier.

Japanese (Windows, DOS)cp932

Shift JIS with Windows characters.

The names are the ones macOS uses. The last two don’t say Shift JIS, but both are based on it. If you’re not sure which to use, choose Japanese (Shift JIS).
