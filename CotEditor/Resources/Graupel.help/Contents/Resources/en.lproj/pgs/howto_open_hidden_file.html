Open hidden files

Some files, such as .gitignore or .zshrc, are hidden in the Finder. Graupel can show them so you can open them.

  • In the Open dialog: Choose File > Open, then select Show invisible files.

  • In a folder’s sidebar: Control-click in the sidebar and choose Show Hidden Files. Choose it again to hide them.
