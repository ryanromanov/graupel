General settings

Choose Graupel > Settings, then click General.

SettingWhat it does
Reopen windows from last session

Opens the documents that were open when you last quit. A change takes effect the next time you start Graupel, or in some cases the time after that. Graupel tells you which when you change it.
When nothing else is open

What Graupel does when it starts with no documents to open: Create New Document, Show Open Dialog, or Do Nothing.
Enable Auto Save with Versions

Saves documents as you work and keeps earlier versions you can go back to. When it’s off, files change only when you save, and Graupel keeps a backup of unsaved changes in case it quits unexpectedly. Graupel asks to restart when you change this. See Save documents.
When document is changed by another application

What happens when another app, such as Git or a formatter, changes a file you have open:

  • Keep Graupel’s version leaves your text alone. Graupel asks before overwriting the other app’s change when you save.

  • Ask how to resolve asks whether to keep your text or load the new file.

  • Update to modified version loads the new file. This also clears the undo history.
Settings import & export

Export… and Import… save and load your settings, and Keep a copy in iCloud keeps a copy for your other Macs. See Move your settings to another Mac.
Dialog warnings

Click Manage Warnings… to turn off warnings you don’t want (see below).

Turn off warnings

Click Manage Warnings…, then select the warnings to stop showing.

  • Inconsistent line endings stops the warning when you open a document with mixed line endings. Turn it off only if you often open such files on purpose; otherwise mixed line endings go unnoticed. See Mixed line endings.
