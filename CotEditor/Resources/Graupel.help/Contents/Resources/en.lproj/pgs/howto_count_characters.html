Count characters

The status bar and the Document inspector show how many characters, lines, and words a document has, and how many are selected.

Graupel counts characters the way you see them. An emoji such as 1️⃣ is made of three Unicode characters (U+0031 U+FE0F U+20E3) but counts as one, the same as the letter A. Unicode calls these grapheme clusters.

Count another way

To count another way, such as in bytes of a text encoding, or without whitespace or line endings:

  1. Choose Text > Advanced Character Count.

  2. Choose what to count, then click Start.

A counter appears at the bottom of the editor and updates as you type. It counts the selection if there is one, or else the whole document. Drag it to move it. The options you choose apply to every document.

Some options take time to count in long documents. To stop counting, choose Text > Stop Advanced Character Count.
