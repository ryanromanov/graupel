Complete words

Graupel suggests words as you type. With a language server, such as the one for Boreas, the suggestions fit the place you’re typing: names from your file, fields after a dot, built-in functions, and keywords. Without one, they come from the words in your document and the document’s syntax.

Complete a word

  1. Type the first letters of a word. After three letters, a list of suggestions appears.

    With a language server, a list can also appear after a dot, such as after the name of a record in Boreas.

  2. Choose a word with the arrow keys and press Return, or keep typing to narrow the list.

To show the list before three letters, press Esc, or choose Edit > Complete (Option-Esc). To close it without choosing a word, press Esc again.

Turn suggestions while typing off or on

Graupel shows suggestions as you type in code. To change this, choose Graupel > Settings, click Mode, select a mode such as Code, and select or deselect Suggest completions while typing. Esc shows the list either way.

In the same settings, Completion list includes sets which words are suggested when there’s no language server.
