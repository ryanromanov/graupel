Insert text from your iPhone or iPad camera

You can take a photo of printed text with your iPhone or iPad, and Graupel inserts the text it reads from the photo. This uses Apple’s Continuity Camera.

  1. Click where you want the text.

  2. Choose Edit > Insert from iPhone or iPad, or Control-click in the document and choose it there. Then choose your device and Take Photo or Scan Documents.

  3. On the device, take the photo and tap Use Photo, or scan the page, tap Keep Scan, then tap Save. If you scan more than one page, Graupel reads only the first.

  4. The image appears on your Mac, with the text Graupel found highlighted. Click Insert.

If the menu lists no devices, make sure your iPhone or iPad is nearby, unlocked, and signed in to the same Apple Account as your Mac. For the full requirements, see the Apple Support article Continuity Camera: Use your iPhone or iPad to scan documents or take a picture on your Mac.
