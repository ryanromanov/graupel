Uninstall Graupel

  1. Quit Graupel.

  2. In the Finder, move Graupel from the Applications folder to the Trash.

This leaves your settings in place, in case you install Graupel again.

Remove your settings too

Delete these files and folders as well. The tilde (~) stands for your home folder. To open a folder, choose Go > Go to Folder in the Finder and type its path.

  • ~/Library/Preferences/com.ryanromanov.Graupel.plist: your settings.

  • ~/Library/Application Support/Graupel/: your themes, syntaxes, and other settings files.

  • ~/Library/Application Scripts/com.ryanromanov.Graupel/: your scripts and language server scripts.

  • /usr/local/bin/graupel: the command-line tool, if you installed it. See Use the graupel command.
