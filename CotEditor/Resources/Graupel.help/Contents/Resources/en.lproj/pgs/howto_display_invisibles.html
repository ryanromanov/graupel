Show invisible characters

Graupel can draw symbols for characters you normally can’t see, such as spaces, tabs, and line endings, so you can tell them apart. The color of the symbols comes from the theme.

Show or hide them in a document

Choose Format > Show Invisibles or Format > Hide Invisibles, or click Invisibles in the toolbar (to add it, see Customize toolbar). This changes only the current document.

Choose which ones Graupel shows

  1. Choose Graupel > Settings, then click Window.

  2. Select Invisible characters to show them in new documents.

  3. Below it, select the kinds to show:
