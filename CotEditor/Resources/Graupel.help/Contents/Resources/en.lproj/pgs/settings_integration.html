Change Integration settings in CotEditor on Mac

In the CotEditor app on your Mac, use Integration settings to review and configure integration with external tools.

To change these settings, choose CotEditor > Settings, then click Integration.

OptionDescription
Command-line tool

Shows the installation status of the cot command-line tool. With cot, you can launch CotEditor and open files from the command line. For details, see Work with cot command.
