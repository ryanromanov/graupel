See a document’s outline

The outline lists the landmarks in a document, such as the headings in Markdown or the functions and types in code. What counts as a landmark depends on the document’s syntax. Click an item to go to it.

In the navigation bar

Choose View > Show Navigation Bar. The bar above the text shows the outline item you’re in. Click it to see the whole list, or choose Find > Open Outline Menu. The arrows next to it go to the previous or next item.

In the inspector

Choose View > Inspector > Outline. The inspector opens on the right with the full outline.

Go to the next item

Choose Find > Select Next Outline Item or Select Previous Outline Item.

Change what appears

The outline rules are part of each syntax. To change them, see Outline rules. Syntaxes that use tree-sitter, such as Swift and Python, have fixed rules.
