Appearance settings

Choose Graupel > Settings, then click Appearance.

SettingWhat it does
Standard font, Monospaced font

The two fonts the editor uses. Click Select… to choose a font and size. Antialias smooths the edges of the letters, and Ligatures joins letter pairs that the font draws as one, such as “fi” or “->” in some coding fonts. Which documents get which font is set in Mode settings. See Change fonts.
Line height

The space from one line to the next, as a multiple of the text size.
Editor opacity

How much of what’s behind the window shows through the editor.
Window theme

The colors of the window around your text: Graupel or Rain, and whether it’s light, dark, or follows your Mac (Match System).
Editor theme

The colors of your text. Match Window uses the window theme; otherwise, choose a theme and Light, Dark, or Match System.
Create or Edit Theme

Opens a list of themes. Select one to change its colors, which appear on the right. The ••• button and Control-click have Duplicate, Rename, Restore, Export…, and Import…. See Change the colors.
