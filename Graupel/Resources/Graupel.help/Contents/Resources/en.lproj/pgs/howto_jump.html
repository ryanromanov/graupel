Go to a specific line

  1. Choose Find > Go to Line.

  2. Type a line number, then press Return or click Go.

To select several lines, type the first line, a colon, and how many lines:

You typeGraupel selects
3

Line 3.

3:2

Two lines starting at line 3: lines 3 and 4.

3:-1

From line 3 to the line before the last. In a 10-line document, lines 3 to 9.
