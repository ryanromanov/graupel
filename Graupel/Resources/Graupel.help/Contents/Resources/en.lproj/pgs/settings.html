Change settings

Choose Graupel > Settings (Command-Comma), then click a pane. To read about the pane you’re in, click the Help button at its bottom.

  • General: startup, saving, changes made by other apps, and moving settings to another Mac.

  • Appearance: fonts, line height, and the color theme.

  • Window: tabs, window size, line numbers, invisible characters, and line wrapping.

  • Edit: indentation, brackets, and other typing behavior.

  • Mode: settings for general documents and for code. See Editor modes.

  • Format: line endings, text encodings, and syntaxes for new and opened documents.

  • Snippets: text you insert with a command or by dropping a file.

  • Shortcuts: keyboard shortcuts for menu commands.

  • Language Servers: the servers that check your code and suggest completions.

  • Integration: the graupel command.

Change the toolbar

Choose View > Customize Toolbar, then drag items into or out of the toolbar.
